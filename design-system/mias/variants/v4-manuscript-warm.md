# V4 — Manuscript Warm

**Dials:** Variance 5/10 · Motion 3/10 · Density 4/10 (standard)
**Base pattern:** Storytelling/hero-centric, reinterpreted from the skill's "Soft UI Evolution" suggestion in the locked palette (its default near-black background is dropped — Light Beige stays dominant here, since a dark UI would fight the "parchment" feeling this variant is going for).

## What's different from MASTER.md

**Hero:** Leans into Light Beige as **parchment**, not just "background." Subtle CSS-generated paper-grain noise texture (an SVG `feTurbulence` filter at very low opacity — no raster texture image, stays low-bandwidth). Marginalia annotations render as actual handwritten-style call-outs in the margin next to the mission statement, in the tentative mono utility face, evoking real manuscript annotation rather than the more abstract "ghosted names" treatment in V1/V2.

**Shape language:** Cards and buttons get a slightly larger border radius (8px vs. MASTER.md's 4px) — softer corners that echo Baloo 2's rounded character, extended consistently into the UI rather than staying only in the display type.

**Layout:** Standard density, generous whitespace, but warmer — section backgrounds alternate between Light Beige and a very subtle Deep-Teal-tinted off-white rather than staying flat throughout.

**Risk profile:** Medium-low. This is the variant most likely to read as approachable to the student/public audience (priority #3 in the PRD) without sacrificing credibility, but it's the one direction where "credible to a historian" and "warm/approachable" are in the most tension — worth a gut-check against the PRD's stated priority order before picking it.

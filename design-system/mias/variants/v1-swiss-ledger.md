**Base pattern:** 12-column Swiss grid, mathematical spacing — closest to the skill's own "Research Lab/University Department" match.

## What's different from MASTER.md

**Hero:** Headline + one-sentence mission statement, set in a strict grid. No ghosted background image at all — the palimpsest concept moves out of the hero and into a **persistent marginal annotation rail**: a narrow column (desktop only, collapses to inline footnotes on mobile) running down the page's right edge, showing dated historical cross-references in the tentative mono utility face ("1885 — Berlin Conference," "1957 — Ghana independence") next to whichever section is in view. It's the most literal, least atmospheric take on "history layered beneath the present."

**Layout:** Everything aligns to a 12-col grid with visible rhythm — focus-area cards are exactly grid-aligned, no offset/asymmetry. Section spacing uses `--space-3xl` (96px) consistently.

**Motion:** Near-none. A single opacity fade on scroll for the marginalia rail entries (matches MASTER.md's scroll-reveal token), nothing else moves.

**Risk profile:** Lowest. This is the "would a historian respect this" answer taken literally — it reads as a serious, slightly severe academic site. Trade-off: least memorable of the five: the signature (marginalia rail) is intellectually interesting but visually quiet, easy to miss on a fast scroll.

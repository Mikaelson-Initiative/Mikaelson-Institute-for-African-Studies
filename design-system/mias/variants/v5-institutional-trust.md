# V5 — Institutional Trust

**Dials:** Variance 3/10 · Motion 2/10 · Density 5/10 (standard)
**Base pattern:** The skill's own "Trust & Authority + Conversion" pattern (Hero mission/credibility → Proof → Solution overview → CTA), taken closest to as-is of any variant here — it's the pattern literally built for "prove credibility to an institutional evaluator."

## What's different from MASTER.md

**Hero:** Plainest of the five — mission statement, one supporting sentence, single CTA. The palimpsest concept is reduced to a **very subtle static background texture only** (ghosted names at ~4% opacity, no parallax, no glass panel) — present for brand consistency with the other pages, but deliberately not the star of this page.

**Proof section (replaces "latest volume/news" framing):** Structured explicitly as credibility signals for the universities/grant-bodies audience — editorial board affiliations, the Mikaelson Initiative relationship stated plainly (not just a footer credit), any confirmed academic partnerships. This is the one variant that most directly optimizes for PRD audience #2 over #1 or #3.

**Layout:** Focus areas as a clean horizontal list with icons, not cards — less visual weight given to any single element, consistent with "nothing here should look like it's trying too hard."

**Risk profile:** Lowest of all five alongside V1, but converges harder on generic "credible institution" territory — the `frontend-design` skill would flag this as the safest choice but the one with the least "could not be mistaken for anyone else's" quality. Best if the immediate priority is university/grant credibility above all else, even at some cost to distinctiveness.
